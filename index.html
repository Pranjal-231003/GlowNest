About GlowNest

Meet Divya, founder of GlowNest

By profession, a chartered accountant. By passion, a creator of calm, cozy spaces. GlowNest began with a simple idea — to craft candles that bring warmth, comfort, and a little thoughtfulness into everyday moments.

Each candle is hand-poured with care and intention, in small batches, from a home studio. Nothing is mass-produced, and every jar is checked by hand before it ships.

Thank you for supporting a small beginning.
— Divya, Founder
