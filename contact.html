Return & Refund Policy

Because each candle is hand-poured in small batches, we do not accept standard returns or exchanges for change of mind once an order has been dispatched.

Damaged in transit or incorrect order?
If your jar arrives broken, damaged in transit, or you received an incorrect pour:
  1. Email us at glownestscentedcandles@gmail.com within 48 hours of delivery.
  2. Include your Order ID, a quick description of the issue, and clear photos of the package and item.
  3. Divya will manually review your request and contact you directly to arrange an immediate free replacement pour or a manual refund to your original payment method.
